# Supporting material for the Master's thesis

**Evaluating the Effectiveness of Chain-of-Thought Explanations in Large Language Models: An Empirical Study of User Understanding, Trust, and Perceived Transparency**

This repository contains selected supporting material for the 2026 Master's thesis submitted to Blekinge Institute of Technology.

## Contents

- `thesis/Final_Thesis.pdf` — thesis manuscript.
- `analysis/computed_statistics.txt` — participant-level summary statistics and test results reported in the study.
- `prompts.txt` — the standard and Chain-of-Thought generation prompts.
- `questions.txt` — the ten experimental questions.
- `survey_instrument.txt` — the blank survey instrument, without participant responses.

## Study overview

The study used a quantitative within-participant design with 25 participants. Each participant evaluated standard and Chain-of-Thought-style responses to ten reasoning-related questions using six five-point Likert-scale items. Analyses included descriptive statistics, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Holm adjustment, effect sizes, Cronbach's alpha, and Spearman correlation.

## Privacy and data availability

Raw participant-level survey exports are intentionally not included. The consent text covered academic research use and did not provide for public release; combinations of timestamps and background responses could also create a re-identification risk. The blank survey instrument and summary statistics are provided instead. The thesis states that derived analysis data, analysis scripts, and generated response materials are available from the author on reasonable request, subject to the original consent.

## Reproducibility note

The statistics summary is included, but the participant-level `derived_data.csv` and executable analysis scripts were not among the files supplied for this upload. Therefore, this repository does not yet independently reproduce the reported statistics.

## Generated response material

The supplied ChatGPT session extracts are partial, out of order, and contain page scaffolding. They are not included as study response data. The repository can be updated when the twenty complete response files (`standard_q01.txt`–`standard_q10.txt` and `cot_q01.txt`–`cot_q10.txt`) are available.
