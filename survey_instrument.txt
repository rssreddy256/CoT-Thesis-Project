SURVEY INSTRUMENT (BLANK)
=========================

Administered through Google Forms. This is the instrument as presented, with no
participant responses. Three background items, then two blocks of ten evaluation
items. Participants were shown the response material as two PDF attachments.


INTRODUCTION AND CONSENT
------------------------

   Evaluation of AI-Generated Responses and Explanations

   This survey is part of a Master's thesis study investigating how different
   types of AI-generated responses influence users' understanding, trust, and
   perception of transparency.

   You will be asked to review AI-generated responses to several
   reasoning-related questions and provide your opinions about the responses.

   Participation is voluntary. The survey takes approximately 10-15 minutes to
   complete. No personally identifiable information will be collected, and the
   responses will be used only for academic research purposes.


BACKGROUND ITEMS  (all mandatory)
---------------------------------

1. How frequently do you use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or
   similar systems?
      Daily / Several times a week / Once a week / Occasionally / Rarely

2. How would you describe your experience with AI tools?
      Beginner / Basic / Intermediate / Advanced / Expert

3. What is your academic or professional background?
      Computer Science / IT
      Data Science / AI
      Engineering
      Other technical field
      Non-technical field
      Other

No other demographic information was collected. Gender, age and country were
not asked.


PRESENTATION OF THE RESPONSE MATERIAL
-------------------------------------

   You will be presented with AI-generated responses to several questions.
   Please read each response carefully and evaluate it based on your own
   perception. There are no right or wrong answers for the evaluation
   questions.

The questionnaire then presented two blocks of ten evaluation items each, headed
"Response A" and "Response B". Response A contained the Chain-of-Thought
responses and Response B the standard responses. The headings gave no indication
of which block was which, so participants were blind to the condition; the order
of the blocks was the same for every participant.


EVALUATION ITEMS
----------------

Each of the twenty responses was rated on the same six statements. Item stem:

   "Please indicate how much you agree with the following statements about the
   response you just read."

   1. The response was easy to understand.
   2. I clearly understood how the answer was obtained.
   3. I trust the answer provided by the AI system.
   4. I would feel confident relying on this answer.
   5. The response made the reasoning behind the answer transparent.
   6. The explanation was clear and useful.

Scale:
   1 - Strongly Disagree
   2 - Disagree
   3 - Neither Agree nor Disagree
   4 - Agree
   5 - Strongly Agree

The questionnaire contained no open-ended items.
