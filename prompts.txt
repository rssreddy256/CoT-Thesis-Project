GENERATION PROMPTS
==================

Both conditions were generated with GPT-5 mini through the ChatGPT web
interface. Each condition used one session, with the prompt below held fixed
across all ten questions in that session. Decoding parameters such as
temperature were not exposed by the interface and are therefore not recorded.


STANDARD CONDITION
------------------

   You are participating in an academic research experiment on AI-generated
   responses.

   Answer the following question clearly and concisely.

   Requirements:
   1. Provide the final answer directly.
   2. Do not provide a step-by-step reasoning process.
   3. Do not provide an extended explanation.
   4. Do not introduce information that is not required to answer the
      question.

   Question: [question text]


CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT CONDITION
--------------------------

   You are participating in an academic research experiment on AI-generated
   responses.

   Answer the following question and provide a clear, step-by-step explanation
   that helps a user understand how the answer was obtained.

   Requirements:
   1. Explain the reasoning clearly and logically.
   2. Present the relevant intermediate steps needed to understand the answer.
   3. Do not introduce irrelevant information.
   4. Clearly state the final answer at the end.

   Question: [question text]


NOTE ON THE DIFFERENCE
----------------------

The two prompts share the same opening sentence and differ in what they require
of the response. The standard prompt contains three prohibitions: no
step-by-step reasoning, no extended explanation, and no unrequired information.
The CoT prompt requires reasoning and intermediate steps. Both prohibit
irrelevant information.

This means the brevity of the standard responses was imposed by instruction
rather than arising incidentally, and that the two conditions differ in response
length and level of detail as well as in the presence of an explanation. This is
stated as a limitation in the thesis (caveat C1, Section 5.2).
