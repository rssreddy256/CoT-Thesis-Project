EXPERIMENTAL QUESTIONS
======================

The ten questions presented to participants. Each was answered twice by
the same model, once under the standard prompt and once under the
Chain-of-Thought prompt (see prompts.txt).

Q1  [Mathematical Reasoning]
   A bat and a ball cost EUR 1.10 in total. The bat costs EUR 1.00 more than
   the ball. How much does the ball cost?

Q2  [Logical Reasoning]
   All researchers in a laboratory have access to the laboratory's database.
   Some people who have access to the database are interns. Can we conclude
   that some researchers in the laboratory are interns? Explain your answer.

Q3  [Commonsense / Situational Reasoning]
   A doctor gives a patient three tablets and tells the patient to take one
   tablet every 30 minutes. How long will it take the patient to take all
   three tablets? Explain your answer.

Q4  [Social and Interpersonal Reasoning]
   A colleague who is normally very talkative becomes unusually quiet during
   a team meeting. What are some possible reasons for this behaviour, and
   how should you respond appropriately?

Q5  [Multi-Step Mathematical Reasoning]
   A jacket is originally priced at EUR 200. The store offers a 25%
   discount. During a special promotion, an additional 10% discount is
   applied to the discounted price. What is the final price of the jacket?

Q6  [Deductive Reasoning]
   Every employee who has completed the security training is allowed to
   access the company's internal system. Daniel is not allowed to access the
   internal system. Can we conclude that Daniel has not completed the
   security training? Explain your answer.

Q7  [Cause-and-Effect Reasoning]
   A company introduces a new software system, and employee productivity
   decreases during the following month. Can the company conclude that the
   new software system caused the decrease in productivity? What other
   factors should be considered?

Q8  [Scenario-Based Decision-Making]
   You are managing a software project that is one week away from its
   deadline. Two team members become unavailable, and three important tasks
   are still incomplete. What should the project manager consider when
   deciding which tasks to prioritize?

Q9  [Ambiguous / Uncertain Reasoning]
   John went to the bank after work and returned home two hours later than
   usual. What was the reason for John's delay?

Q10 [Risk and Evidence Evaluation]
   A social media post claims that a new medicine can completely prevent a
   particular disease. What information should you consider before deciding
   whether the claim is trustworthy?
